# Home battery storage

A home battery keeps surplus solar power for the evening. Batteries are sold as modules that stack in a cabinet.

## Capacity

One battery module stores 10 kWh usable. To store a given amount of energy, divide it by 10 kWh and round up to whole modules.

## Efficiency

Round trip efficiency of a home battery is about 90 percent, so a module returns roughly 9 kWh of the 10 kWh put in.
