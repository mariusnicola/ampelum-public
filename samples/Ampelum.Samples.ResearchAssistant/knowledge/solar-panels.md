# Rooftop solar panels

Photovoltaic panels turn sunlight directly into electricity. The size of an array is given in kilowatts peak (kW), the output it reaches under standard test light.

## Yield

In Central Europe a rooftop array makes about 1,100 kWh per kW peak per year. South facing roofs at a 30 degree tilt reach the top of that range, east and west roofs lose about 15 percent.

## Ageing

Panels need little care. Rain clears most dust, and the output of the cells falls by about 0.5 percent per year.
