# Small wind turbines

A small wind turbine on a mast turns moving air into electricity. Its rated power is the output at the design wind speed.

## Yield

A 5 kW turbine at a site with a capacity factor of 20 percent makes about 8,760 kWh per year. Sheltered sites by trees or houses do much worse.

## Noise

Blade noise grows with wind speed. Keep a mast at least 100 meters from neighbours.
